How to brew better filter coffee at home

Most people upgrade their kettle before they fix their ratio. Start with the three variables that change the cup the most.

1. Ratio

Use 60 g of coffee per litre of water as a baseline (1:16). Weigh both; volume is not reliable.

2. Grind

Aim for coarse sand. If the brew tastes sour and thin, go finer; if it is bitter and dry, go coarser.

3. Water temperature

Between 92 and 96 °C. If you have no thermometer, let the kettle rest 30 seconds after boiling.

Change one thing at a time and taste. That is the whole method.
