Meet the farmers behind our Ethiopian lot

Our most floral coffee comes from a cooperative of about 300 smallholders in the Guji highlands, growing at 1,900 to 2,200 metres.

Cherries are hand-picked, sorted and dried on raised beds for two to three weeks. The result is a cup with jasmine, bergamot and a honey-like sweetness.

We pay a fixed premium per kilo above the market price and visit every other year. This season's lot is in the Regular and Office plans through March.
